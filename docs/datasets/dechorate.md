# dEchorate

`dechorate` · measured · 2021 · wave 2 · status **planned**

1800 annotated RIRs from 6 arrays of 5 microphones, 6 sources and 11 acoustic conditions (reflective panels on/off) of one room; echo timings annotated (processed RIR file only).
