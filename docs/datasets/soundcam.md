# SoundCam

`soundcam` · measured · 2023 · wave 2 · status **planned**

5,000 10-channel real-world RIRs in three rooms (treated lab, in-the-wild living room, conference room) with humans at positions throughout each room. Only the Conference Room archive is fetched for now.

## Provenance and terms

- Source: http https://stacks.stanford.edu/file/druid:xq364hd5023/ConferenceRoom_preprocessed.tar.gz
- Homepage: https://sites.google.com/view/soundcam
- Paper: [SoundCam: A Dataset for Tasks in Tracking and Identifying Humans from Real Room Acoustics](https://nips.cc/virtual/2023/poster/73471)
- Licence: MIT
- Audio redistribution: **yes** · ML training use: **allowed**
- Notes: Preprocessed archives total 413 GB (RIRs bundled with music recordings); Stanford serves ~2 MB/s. Only ConferenceRoom_preprocessed (51.7 GB) is fetched; LivingRoom (104 GB), TreatedRoom (211 GB) and TreatedRoomPanels (46 GB) are deferred until permanent storage.
