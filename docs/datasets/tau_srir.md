# TAU Spatial Room Impulse Response Database

`tau_srir` · measured · 2022 · wave 2 · status **planned**

SRIRs from 9 spaces of Tampere University (fixed receiver, many source positions along trajectories), first-order Ambisonics and tetrahedral array formats derived from Eigenmike em32, downsampled to 24 kHz.
