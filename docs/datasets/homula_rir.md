# HOMULA-RIR

`homula_rir` · measured · 2024 · wave 2 · status **planned**

Seminar-room RIRs from a 64-microphone ULA (eStick) near 2 Genelec 8020 speakers, and higher-order microphones (Voyage Spatial Mic) at 25 attendee positions (teleconferencing scenario).
