# SRIR dataset: a robot's journey through coupled rooms

`aalto_robot_coupled` · measured · 2024 · wave 2 · status **planned**

SRIRs captured by a robot with a microphone array moving through coupled rooms (corridors, halls, an office) of a reverberant university building; two Zenodo parts (2024, 2026 office).
