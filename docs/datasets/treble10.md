# Treble10-RIR

`treble10` · simulated · 2025 · wave 4 · status **deferred**

3000+ broadband RIRs from a hybrid wave-based (<5 kHz) + geometrical solver in 10 furnished real-world rooms (bathrooms, bedrooms, living rooms, meeting rooms), receiver grids at 3 heights.
