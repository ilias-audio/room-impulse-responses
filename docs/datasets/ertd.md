# Extended Room Transition Dataset (ERTD)

`ertd` · simulated · 2024 · wave 4 · status **planned**

2,833 FDTD-simulated RIRs per variant on a 0.2 m grid over a meeting room coupled to a hallway (the geometry of McKenzie's Room Transition Dataset, NLOS source), at 1.55 m height; ERTD1 absorption matched to the measurements, ERTD2 with a strongly absorbing hallway wall.
