# Multi-Room Transition Dataset (MRTD)

`aalto_multiroom` · measured · 2024 · wave 2 · status **active**

RIRs measured along paths through 3 complex multi-room environments (hallways + lecture hall, offices, workshops) with 4 fixed loudspeakers and a mobile platform carrying a GRAS 45BB KEMAR and a Zoom H3-VR tetrahedral array; 48 kHz. Aimed at multi-slope energy decay.
