# TU Ilmenau SRIR dataset: a robot's journey through coupled rooms

`tuil_robot_coupled` · measured · 2024 · wave 2 · status **active**

SRIRs measured by a robot carrying a 7-mic SDM array (6 EM258 capsules at r = 5 cm plus a centre Earthworks M30 omni) on a grid through coupled spaces of TU Ilmenau's Helmholtzbau (hallway, coffee corner, two staircases; 2024) and along 4 lines from an office into the hallway (office part). 754 positions; 2 or 3 loudspeakers, each measured 2-3 times (first repeat indexed).
