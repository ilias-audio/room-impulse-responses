# AcousticRooms

`acousticrooms` · simulated · 2025 · wave 4 · status **deferred**

300,000+ single-channel RIRs simulated in 260 rooms of 10 categories (apartment, auditorium, office, cafe, ...) with 3D geometry and materials from a 332-material library.
